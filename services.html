Classics already in India

Where NHM starts

For cars already in India, the first step would be checking identity, condition and paperwork. Any work would follow an agreed scope and budget.

  1. Reconditioned cars for sale

    We plan to find Japanese classics that are already registered in India, buy them once their papers check out, recondition them and sell them with a full file:

    • import papers, such as the bill of entry and proof of customs duty paid, checked
    • registration certificate and ownership history
    • chassis and engine numbers matched to the papers
    • reconditioning work recorded, with photographs
    • what is original and what isn't, stated plainly

  2. Documentation for owners

    Many older imports have papers spread across owners, offices and states. The proposed record would assemble available import documents, registration history and service records, mark gaps, and identify questions for specialist review.

  3. Service and reconditioning

    From routine servicing to a full recommissioning after years off the road:

    • inspection and a written report before any work
    • mechanical, electrical, bodywork and trim reconditioning
    • original and reproduction parts sourced from Japan
    • a record of every job, added to the car's file

  4. Re-registration

    Depending on the car and state, registration work may involve:

    • transfer of ownership
    • moving the registration to another state, with a no-objection certificate from the original authority
    • renewal of registration and fitness for older cars
    • vintage registration, where the car qualifies

    Approval rests with the authority, and requirements vary by state.

Our intended standard is to verify import and registration records before any future purchase, sale or work, and to disclose unresolved gaps.

Importing from Japan

Future Japan import concept

This proposed service is not available yet. If developed, the buyer would purchase directly; NHM would coordinate research, inspection and import support.

  1. Find the car

    If developed, this service could begin with a model, condition and budget brief, followed by research into eligible cars and independent inspection before any purchase.

  2. Inspect it before you buy

    An independent inspection should cover:

    • the underside, floors, sills and suspension mounts
    • chassis and engine numbers, checked against the papers
    • a cold start and the engine running
    • bodywork, paint and signs of accident repair
    • service records and Japanese registration papers

  3. You buy it, with us beside you

    A future brief would set out the buyer, seller, fees and estimated landed cost before a purchase. A licensed broker should confirm the applicable import conditions.

  4. Ship it to India

    A future import plan would account for:

    • collection from the seller and transport to the port in Japan
    • deregistration and the export certificate
    • container shipping and marine insurance
    • customs clearance at the Indian port, through a licensed broker
    • vintage registration with your transport authority

  5. Service and maintain it

    After arrival, care could include:

    • check it over on arrival and fix anything the journey turned up
    • service it regularly and keep a record of the work
    • source original and reproduction parts from Japan
    • help with restoration when it's needed

Questions

Do you sell cars?
Not yet. We may later offer documented cars already registered in India. The model profiles are research only.
Can you help with a car I already own?
Not yet. Our proposed approach would begin with a review of the car, its records and the available specialists. Registration decisions remain with the relevant authority.
Why do I buy the car myself?
The import category is for actual users. Buying in your own name may fit that requirement, but your broker must verify the complete import and registration conditions for the specific car.
Which cars can I import?
A specific car may qualify if it is more than 50 years old from first registration and meets the other vintage conditions. Use the eligibility checker.
What will it cost?
Duty and tax usually add more than the car's price. Use the landed-cost calculator with quoted rates. Our fees will be published when we open.
