Services

A concierge for Japanese classics

NHM doesn't sell cars. You choose your car and buy it in your own name. We help you find the right one, inspect it before you commit, ship it to India, and look after it once it's home.

NHM is launching. Partners in Japan and India are being put in place, and we aren't taking clients yet.

What we do

  1. Find the car

    Tell us the model, condition and budget you have in mind. We search dealer auctions, specialist dealers and collectors in Japan. You get a shortlist of cars that are old enough to import to India, with translated auction sheets and our notes on each.

  2. Inspect it before you buy

    Before you bid or pay, an inspector sees the car in person. You receive a written report with photographs covering:

    • the underside, floors, sills and suspension mounts
    • chassis and engine numbers, checked against the papers
    • a cold start and the engine running
    • bodywork, paint and signs of accident repair
    • service records and Japanese registration papers

  3. You buy it, with us beside you

    You buy the car in your own name and pay the seller directly. India's vintage import policy is for the car's actual user, so the car is yours from the start. Before you commit, we explain the auction sheet, the fees and the likely landed cost.

  4. Ship it to India

    Once you've paid, we take care of getting the car home:

    • collection from the seller and transport to the port in Japan
    • deregistration and the export certificate
    • container shipping and marine insurance
    • customs clearance at the Indian port, through a licensed broker
    • vintage registration with your transport authority

  5. Service and maintain it

    A fifty-year-old car needs people who know it. Once it's home, we:

    • check it over on arrival and fix anything the journey turned up
    • service it regularly and keep a record of the work
    • source original and reproduction parts from Japan
    • help with restoration when it's needed

Questions

Do you sell cars?
No. You buy the car directly from the seller in Japan. The listings are profiles of cars we can help you find, not stock.
Why do I buy the car myself?
India's vintage import policy is for the car's actual user, and restricts resale. Buying in your own name keeps the car yours and the import straightforward.
Which cars can I import?
Cars that are at least 50 years old. The cutoff rolls forward each year. Use the eligibility checker.
What will it cost?
Duty and tax usually add more than the car's price. Use the landed-cost calculator with quoted rates. Our fees will be published when we open.
