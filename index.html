Why Japanese classics

Find a different story

Your fellow founders already own Porsches. The Fairlady Z, the Toyota 2000GT and the first Skyline GT-R were built with the same obsession, and they're still rarely seen on Indian roads.

Buy one we've already brought back to its best, or import your own. Either way, we make sure it's the right car, with its papers in order, and keep it on the road.

Already in India

Japanese classics that are already here

Over the years, many Japanese cars were imported into India and registered here. Some have incomplete files; others need careful work. This is where NHM starts: finding these cars, putting their papers in order, reconditioning them and offering them for sale, and doing the same work for owners.

  • Buy A reconditioned classic, with its file We plan to buy registered cars once their papers check out, recondition them and sell them with a documented history.
  • Document Put your car's papers in order Import papers, registration history and service records in one file, with any gaps marked and a plan to fill them.
  • Service and re-register Recondition it and keep it legal Servicing and reconditioning by people who know these cars, plus ownership transfer and re-registration where required.

We only buy, sell or work on cars whose import and registration papers can be verified. If a car's papers don't add up, we'll tell you what we found before any work or sale.

    Importing from Japan

    From auction hall to your garage

    You would stay the buyer and owner. Our proposed concierge service would coordinate the surrounding steps. NHM is launching, and partners in Japan and India are being put in place.

    1. 01

      Find

      Tell us what you're after. We search auctions, dealers and collectors in Japan and shortlist cars that are old enough to import.

    2. 02

      Inspect

      Before you bid, an inspector sees the car in person and reports on the underside, engine, bodywork and papers.

    3. 03

      You buy

      You buy the car in your own name, as India's vintage import rules require. We explain the auction sheet and every fee first.

    4. 04

      Ship

      We plan to coordinate collection, export papers, shipping, customs clearance and an application for vintage registration in India; approval rests with the authorities.

    5. 05

      Maintain

      Once it's home, we service and maintain the car and source parts from Japan.
