Japanese classics · beginning in Goa

Preserve the car. Preserve its story.

Nihon Heritage Motors is being developed around Japanese classics already in India. Beginning in Goa, we aim to inspect and document each car before deciding whether it needs work or a new custodian.

Our approach Tell us about a car

Why Japanese classics

Find a different story

The Fairlady Z, Toyota 2000GT and first Skyline GT-R are part of Japan’s rich automotive history. Few examples are seen on Indian roads.

Before work begins, we want to understand what a car is, what has changed and what remains uncertain.

Already in India

Japanese classics that are already here

We plan to begin in Goa with Japanese classics already in India. Inspection and documentation would guide any later work or sale.

  • Buy A reconditioned classic, with its file We plan to buy registered cars once their papers check out, recondition them and sell them with a documented history.
  • Document Put your car's papers in order Import papers, registration history and service records in one file, with any gaps marked and a plan to fill them.
  • Service and re-register Recondition it and keep it legal Servicing and reconditioning by people who know these cars, plus ownership transfer and re-registration where required.

Our intended standard is to verify import and registration records before any future purchase, sale or work, and to disclose unresolved gaps.
