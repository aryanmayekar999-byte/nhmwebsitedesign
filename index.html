Why Japanese classics

Find a different story

Your fellow founders already own Porsches. The Fairlady Z, the Skyline GT-R and the first NSX were built with the same obsession and are still rarely seen on Indian roads.

NHM is for collectors who want a car with a story they can tell, and paperwork to prove it.

How it will work

From auction hall to your garage

This is the process we're setting up. Sourcing, inspection and import partners in Japan and India aren't in place yet.

  1. 01

    Source

    Search auctions and private collections against your brief, and shortlist the cars worth a second look.

  2. 02

    Inspect

    Have an inspector see each car in person and photograph the underside, the engine bay and every panel gap.

  3. 03

    Document

    Translate the auction sheet, service books and export certificate, and bind them into one file.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Arrange shipping to India, customs clearance and registration with licensed partners.

Provenance

A car is only as good as its paperwork

The file we intend to put together for every car, in plain English:

Auction sheet, translated
Grade, inspector's notes and the condition map, line by line.
Ownership and service
Every record we can recover, in date order, with gaps marked.
Export and import
Deregistration, export certificate and a landed-cost statement.
