    The concierge

    From auction hall to your garage

    You stay the buyer and the owner. We handle everything around the purchase. NHM is launching, and partners in Japan and India are being put in place.

    1. 01

      Find

      Tell us what you're after. We search auctions, dealers and collectors in Japan and shortlist cars that are old enough to import.

    2. 02

      Inspect

      Before you bid, an inspector sees the car in person and reports on the underside, engine, bodywork and papers.

    3. 03

      You buy

      You buy the car in your own name, as India's vintage import rules require. We explain the auction sheet and every fee first.

    4. 04

      Ship

      We arrange collection, export papers, shipping, customs clearance and vintage registration in India.

    5. 05

      Maintain

      Once it's home, we service and maintain the car and source parts from Japan.
