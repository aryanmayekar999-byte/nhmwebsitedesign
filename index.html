Import lane

From auction hall to your garage

Four stages, one person responsible for your car at each of them.

  1. 01

    Source

    We search auctions and private collections against your brief, and send you the cars worth a second look.

  2. 02

    Inspect

    An inspector sees the car in person and photographs the underside, the engine bay and every panel gap.

  3. 03

    Document

    Auction sheets, service books and export certificates are translated and bound into one file.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    We handle shipping, duties and registration, and hand over the keys with the file.
