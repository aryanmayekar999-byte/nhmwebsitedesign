American classics, up to 1976

Luxury cruisers from Cadillac, Lincoln, Imperial and Packard, Chrysler’s 300 letter cars, and America’s great sports and muscle cars. India allows a car to be imported as vintage once it’s 50 years old, and the cutoff rolls forward every year. These are profiles of cars we can help you find, not stock for sale.

    Left-hand drive

    American cars are left-hand drive, and India drives on the left. Before you buy, ask your broker and your state transport authority how a left-hand-drive vintage car is registered and where it may be driven. We check this with you at the brief stage.
