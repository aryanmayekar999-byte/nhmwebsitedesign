Privacy policy

This policy describes how Woodward Avenue by NHM handles information when you use this informational website. Last updated 26 September 2026.

Who to contact

Woodward Avenue by NHM is the name used for this website. For privacy questions or requests, email aryanmayekar999@gmail.com. This is a preview site; the business identity for any future paid service will be provided before an engagement begins.

Information you provide

There is no account, checkout or enquiry form on this site. If you email us, we receive your address and the information you include. We use it to respond to your message and handle any related request. Please do not email financial, identity or vehicle documents unless we have agreed a secure way to receive them.

Hosting and external services

Our hosting providers may process IP addresses, request details and security logs to deliver and protect the site. The pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, whose servers may receive your IP address and browser request. On pages with the currency guide, your browser requests daily reference rates from Exchange Rate API, which may receive your IP address and browser request. The rate response is cached in your browser for up to 24 hours; amounts you type are calculated locally and are not sent to the rate provider. Links to other websites are governed by those sites’ own policies. We do not currently run advertising trackers or a mailing list.

Retention and requests

We keep email correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to answer and manage it, or to meet applicable obligations. Hosting logs follow the provider’s retention settings. To ask about, correct or delete information you sent by email, contact aryanmayekar999@gmail.com. We will respond under applicable law. If our collection or providers change, we will update this policy.
