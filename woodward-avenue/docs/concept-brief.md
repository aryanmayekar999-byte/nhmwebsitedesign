# Woodward Avenue by NHM — identity concept

## Position

Woodward Avenue is the proposed American motoring-heritage expression within NHM. Its name evokes Detroit's historic avenue and the culture of American cars, while the small “by NHM” endorsement connects it to the parent brand. This is a concept identity, not evidence of a showroom, stock or operating service.

## Visual idea

- A broad uppercase Art Deco wordmark gives the name the presence of a period dealership sign.
- Tapered parallel rules beneath the name suggest an avenue converging toward a horizon. They should remain abstract, without becoming a road sign.
- A single red dot links back to the existing NHM identity. It is the only vivid accent.
- The parent endorsement remains legible, but secondary. The Woodward Avenue name should work on its own from a distance.

## Typography and production

Use a custom drawn or carefully adjusted geometric display serif for the wordmark. Use a restrained serif for “by NHM” and a readable modern sans serif for ordinary body copy. Do not substitute the rendered wordmark with an off-the-shelf font without adjusting the distinctive W and letter spacing.

For print and signage, redraw the selected concept as a clean vector master; the generated PNG is for direction only. Make light and dark variants and test the copper rules and endorsement at business-card size. Avoid blind emboss as the sole treatment on dark stock. Preserve original automaker badges on vehicles; use Woodward Avenue plaques on separate display furniture or documents.

## Mockups

The stationery tests dark ink on ivory and ivory ink on charcoal. The workshop sign tests distance legibility. The metal plaque tests a small, physical expression beside a preserved American car. They are conceptual scenes and do not represent an existing NHM premises or inventory.

## Generation prompts used

The logo prompt requested a transparent horizontal Art Deco “WOODWARD AVENUE” wordmark, an abstract avenue motif, charcoal/copper palette, one NHM red dot and a small “by NHM” endorsement, excluding wings, flags, shields and car silhouettes. The three mockup prompts applied that logo to uncoated stationery, a charcoal workshop fascia, and an etched display plaque beside an American classic.
