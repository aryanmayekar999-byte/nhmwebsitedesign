Services

Everything NHM does, for American steel.

The same service that brings heritage cars out of Japan, pointed at the chrome, V8s and long hoods of Detroit and beyond. One team handles the whole journey, and tells you plainly what it finds.

01

Sourcing

We find the car you’re after through US auctions, specialist dealers and private collections, and shortlist only what stands up.

02

Pre-purchase inspection

Someone on the ground checks body, frame, drivetrain and numbers before you commit, with photographs of everything.

03

Provenance & records

VIN and trim-tag decoding, title and ownership history, and build documents where they survive, gathered into one file.

04

Landed-cost estimate

A written estimate of what the car costs at your door, freight, duties and fees included, before you decide.

05

Import & shipping

Export paperwork, ocean freight, customs clearance and compliance, handled end to end by the NHM import team.

06

Delivery & handover

Enclosed delivery to your door, and a walk-through of the car and its file with the person who inspected it.

Original badges stay on the car. Ours goes on the stand.

Every car keeps its factory emblems, finishes and numbers. We record what’s original and what isn’t, and we never replace one with the other. The Woodward Avenue name lives on display furniture and paperwork, not on the car.

How it works

Four steps from listing to your driveway.

  1. I

    Tell us the car

    Make, model, year range, budget, and what matters most: numbers-matching, colour, originality or driving condition.

  2. II

    We find and inspect

    We search auctions, dealers and private sellers, then inspect in person and send you an honest report.

  3. III

    We document and ship

    Title, VIN decode and history go into the file while we handle export, freight and customs.

  4. IV

    Delivered, with its file

    The car arrives at your door with its provenance file and a condition report you can hold us to.

Provenance

Every car arrives with its file.

An American classic is only as good as its story. We gather the paperwork before the car leaves the US, so you know exactly what you own.

  • VIN, cowl and trim-tag decode
  • Title and ownership history
  • Build sheet or window sticker, where it survives
  • Inspection report and full photo set
  • Export, freight and customs papers
