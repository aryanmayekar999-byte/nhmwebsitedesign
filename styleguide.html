Design system

Style guide

The tokens and components this site is built from. Values mirror design-system/tokens.json; the rules behind them are in design-system/brand-book.md.

Logo

Use the file as supplied, on paper, warmpaper or washi, at least 48px tall, with space-6 clear space at hero size. Never redraw it or recolour the dot.

Colour

Black and white surfaces, with NHMred as the one accent: at most one red element per view.

  • ink#111111Primary text
  • paper#FFFFFFPage background
  • warmpaper#F7F6F3Cards, panels
  • NHMred#DC010FThe one accent
  • Slate#626262Secondary text
  • Hairline#DEDEDADecorative rules
  • border#767676Control borders
  • washi#E6DDD1Print, feature panels
  • charcoal#2C2927Print card stock
