Woodward Avenue by NHM

The same concierge, for American classics. Woodward Avenue by NHM helps collectors in India find a classic American car, inspect it before they buy, ship it home and keep it running.

Coming soon. Woodward Avenue by NHM will have its own website. We'll add the link here when it launches.

The name

Named for Detroit's great cruising street

Woodward Avenue runs out of Detroit, where America's car industry grew up, and it has been a cruising street for generations. Nihon Heritage Motors is about Japan; Woodward Avenue by NHM is about the cars that came out of Detroit and the rest of America.

How it works

One concierge, two continents

As with NHM, you buy the car in your own name and we handle everything around it: the search, a pre-purchase inspection, shipping to India, customs, registration, and service afterwards. See how the concierge works.

Worth knowing

  • Most American classics are left-hand drive. India drives on the left, so check with your transport authority how a left-hand-drive vintage car can be registered and used before you buy.
  • Shipping is longer. Cars travel from the US east or west coast, so budget more time and freight than for a car from Japan.
  • Where they're sold. American classics are sold through online auctions and specialist dealers, such as Bring a Trailer, Mecum, Barrett-Jackson and Hemmings. They're listed for reference; NHM isn't affiliated with them.
  • The landed cost works the same way. The landed-cost calculator works in yen, so convert the US price and costs to yen before you enter them.
