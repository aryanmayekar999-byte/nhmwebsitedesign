European motoring · an NHM concept

Good cars deserve a better record.

Europa Motoring by NHM is being developed for interesting European cars in India. We intend to select carefully, check condition and history, and describe each car plainly before any future sale.

What we would selectOur approach

The first chapter

Attainable, interesting, worth keeping.

Europa could begin with well kept European cars that enthusiasts can realistically find and own in India. A Volkswagen Polo GT TSI, BMW 1 Series, Mercedes-Benz A-Class, Audi Q3 or Škoda Fabia might be considered when its specification, condition and records justify it. These are examples of our selection thinking, not current stock.

No inventory or vehicle services are available through this site yet. We are developing the selection and documentation process.

Shared NHM standard

The vehicle record comes first.

For a future Europa car, the proposed record would bring together identity and registration documents, service history, inspection findings, photographs, known repairs and modifications. Missing or uncertain information would be marked as such.

Work would be scoped only after inspection. Sound original material should be kept where practical, and any corrections recorded. This is the same discipline NHM intends to apply to Japanese and American cars.

Proposed process

From candidate to custodian.

  1. Find

    Identify a car with a clear reason to consider it.

  2. Inspect

    Check papers, identity, condition and model specific risks.

  3. Document

    Build a record and mark unresolved questions.

  4. Decide

    Plan necessary work and a suitable next step.
