Our approach

Preserve the car. Preserve its story.

Nihon Heritage Motors is developing a measured way to discover and care for Japanese classics already in India, beginning in Goa. These principles will guide each decision when NHM begins operating.

Principles before projects

  • Preserve before restoring. Keep sound original material where possible; a full restoration is not the default.
  • Document before altering. Record a car's condition, numbers, papers, photographs and known history before work begins.
  • Disclose rather than disguise. Make repairs, replacements, modifications and gaps in the record visible.
  • Use specialist judgment. Inspect first and plan only the work that the car needs.
  • Leave a better record. Every future NHM vehicle should carry clearer evidence than when it arrived.

The NHM Vehicle Record

We are designing a record template, not claiming that a completed vehicle history service exists. Depending on available evidence, it would collect identity and registration details, chassis and engine information, factory specification, known ownership, baseline photos, originality and modifications, inspection findings, work performed, parts, invoices and later custodian history. Missing or disputed facts would be marked as such.

A careful sequence

Find → inspect → document → recommission where warranted → preserve → place. Any acquisition or customer work would require a vehicle-specific inspection, scope, budget and suitable specialist before a commitment.
