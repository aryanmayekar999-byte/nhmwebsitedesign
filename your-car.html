Tell us about a car

Every car has a story worth recording.

Know of a Japanese classic already in India, especially in Goa? We welcome an initial introduction while NHM develops its process. We are not yet buying vehicles, accepting consignments or taking on paid work.

What to include

A short note is enough: make and model, approximate year, location, whether it is registered, broad condition and known history, and whether you are the owner or simply know the car. Mention if you are considering a sale or want to share its story. We can discuss next steps only after reviewing the basics.

Please do not email identity documents, registration scans, full chassis numbers or other sensitive records at this stage. We can agree a suitable way to review documents if a conversation develops.
