# Nihon Heritage Motors

**Japanese classics for collectors in India.**

Nihon Heritage Motors (NHM) is a concierge for people in India who want to own a classic Japanese car. We help clients find the right car in Japan, inspect it before they buy, ship it to India, and look after it for as long as they own it.

NHM is launching. Partners in Japan and India are being put in place, and we aren't taking clients yet.

## What NHM is, and isn't

- **NHM is a concierge.** The client chooses the car and buys it in their own name, paying the seller directly. NHM handles everything around the purchase.
- **NHM is not a dealer.** We don't buy cars to resell, hold stock or take ownership of a client's car. The listings on the website are model profiles, not cars for sale.

This model fits India's vintage import policy, which allows a vintage car to be imported by its actual user for personal use and restricts resale.

## Services

| Service | What the client gets |
| --- | --- |
| **Find** | A brief-led search of Japanese dealer auctions, specialist dealers and collectors, with a shortlist of cars that are old enough to import and translated auction sheets. |
| **Inspect** | An in-person pre-purchase inspection with a written, photographed report: underside, chassis and engine numbers, a cold start, bodywork and papers. |
| **Advise on the purchase** | An explanation of the auction sheet, every fee and the likely landed cost before the client commits. |
| **Ship and import** | Collection in Japan, deregistration and export certificate, container shipping and insurance, customs clearance through a licensed broker, and vintage registration in India. |
| **Service and maintain** | An arrival check, regular servicing with records, parts sourced from Japan, and help with restoration. |

## Who it's for

Collectors and first-time classic buyers in India, including founders and professionals who want something more distinctive than the usual European sports car. Our campaign line: *"Your fellow founders already own Porsches. Find a different story."*

## The rules we work within

- **Eligibility:** India allows a car to be imported as vintage once it is 50 years old. The cutoff rolls forward every year, so in 2026 cars built in 1976 or earlier qualify, and in 2027 cars from 1977 join them. This follows DGFT Notification 58/2024-25 (February 2025), which aligned import policy with the vintage definition in the Central Motor Vehicles Rules.
- **Actual user:** the car is imported by the person who will own and use it, without an import licence.
- **Resale:** the policy restricts reselling imported vintage cars in India.
- **Registration:** vintage cars used on public roads are registered as vintage with the state transport authority and have their own registration series and conditions of use.
- **Cost:** customs duty and IGST usually add more than the car's price. NHM quotes the landed cost using rates confirmed by a licensed customs broker.

These are general notes, not legal or tax advice. Confirm current rules with a customs broker before any purchase.

Examples include the Toyota 2000GT, Nissan Skyline 2000GT-R (PGC10/KPGC10 and KPGC110), Datsun 240Z / Fairlady Z, Mazda Cosmo Sport, Honda S800, Mitsubishi Galant GTO, Toyota Celica (TA22), Toyota Corolla Levin (TE27) and Datsun Bluebird 510. ## Brand

NHM should feel like a specialist automotive archive: precise, understated and historically minded. The brand uses black-and-white surfaces with NHM red (`#DC010F`) as the one deliberate accent, Cormorant Garamond headings, Inter text, square corners, and no gradients or racing graphics. The emblem is the N·H·M monogram in a thin ring with a red dot, over NIHON HERITAGE MOTORS.
