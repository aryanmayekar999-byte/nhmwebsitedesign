# Nihon Heritage Motors

**Japanese classics for collectors in India.**

Nihon Heritage Motors (NHM) is for people in India who want to own a classic Japanese car. It works in two ways:

1. **Classics already in India (where NHM starts).** Many Japanese cars were imported into India years ago and have since been registered here. NHM sources these cars, buys them once their papers check out, reconditions them and resells them with a documented history. It also documents, services, reconditions and re-registers cars for their owners.
2. **Importing from Japan.** For a car still in Japan, NHM acts as a concierge: the client buys the car in their own name, and NHM finds it, inspects it, ships it to India and looks after it.

NHM is launching. Partners in Japan and India are being put in place, and we aren't taking clients yet.

## How the two sides differ

- **Cars already in India: NHM buys and sells.** NHM only buys cars already registered in India whose import and registration papers can be verified, and sells them with a full file. If a car's papers don't add up, it doesn't buy it, and it tells an owner what it found before any work.
- **Imports from Japan: NHM is a concierge, not the importer.** The client chooses the car and buys it in their own name, paying the seller directly. This fits India's vintage import policy, which allows a vintage car to be imported by its actual user for personal use and restricts resale. NHM does not import cars to resell them.
- The website listings are model profiles, not stock. Cars for sale will be listed separately once NHM launches.

## The rules we work within

- **Eligibility:** India allows a car to be imported as vintage once it is 50 years old. The cutoff rolls forward every year, so in 2026 cars built in 1976 or earlier qualify, and in 2027 cars from 1977 join them. This follows DGFT Notification 58/2024-25 (February 2025), which aligned import policy with the vintage definition in the Central Motor Vehicles Rules.
- **Actual user:** the car is imported by the person who will own and use it, without an import licence.
- **Resale:** the policy restricts reselling imported vintage cars in India.
- **Registration:** vintage cars used on public roads are registered as vintage with the state transport authority and have their own registration series and conditions of use.
- **Cost:** customs duty and IGST usually add more than the car's price. NHM quotes the landed cost using rates confirmed by a licensed customs broker.

These are general notes, not legal or tax advice. Confirm current rules with a customs broker before any purchase.

## Brand

NHM should feel like a specialist automotive archive: precise, understated and historically minded. The brand uses black-and-white surfaces with NHM red (`#DC010F`) as the one deliberate accent, Cormorant Garamond headings, Inter text, square corners, and no gradients or racing graphics. The emblem is the N·H·M monogram in a thin ring with a red dot, over NIHON HERITAGE MOTORS.
