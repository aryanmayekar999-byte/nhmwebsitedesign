Auctions and exporters

Where Japanese classics are sold

Most used cars in Japan change hands at weekly dealer auctions that overseas buyers can't join directly. You reach them through an auction agent, buy from an exporter's own stock, or bid at a collector auction. Every service below sells to overseas buyers.

Listed for reference. NHM isn't affiliated with any of these companies and hasn't vetted them. Before you pay, confirm that they will ship to India and can prepare the papers for a vintage import. What to check before you pay

Prefer not to do it alone? NHM's concierge searches these sources for you and inspects cars before you buy.

Dealer auctions

The source of most cars

Tens of thousands of cars are sold every week across more than a hundred auction venues. Only licensed members can bid, so overseas buyers use an agent. Each car is graded by an inspector on an auction sheet.

Auction groupWhat to know
USSJapan's largest auction network, with venues across the country and sales every week. Most classics that reach a dealer auction appear here.
TAAToyota's auction group, with a strong supply of Toyota trade-ins.
HAA KobeA large independent auction in western Japan.
JUAuctions run by regional used-car dealer associations.
CAAAuctions in the Chubu region, around Nagoya and Tokyo.
AraiVenues around Tokyo, including specialist sales.

Auction agents

Bid at dealer auctions through an agent

An agent searches the auctions for you, translates auction sheets, bids up to your limit, then handles deregistration, export and shipping. You usually pay a deposit before bidding and the full price within a few working days of winning.

  • Japan Car Direct

    japancardirect.com
    What it is
    An auction agent that bids at Japanese dealer auctions and exports to buyers abroad. It's well known among importers of older JDM cars.
    How you buy
    Register, pay a deposit, send the cars you want to bid on, and set a maximum. They bid, invoice you and arrange shipping.
    Good for
    Finding a specific model at auction with guidance on the auction sheet.
    • Dealer auctions
    • Sheet translation
    • Export

  • Provide Cars

    providecars.co.jp
    What it is
    A Japanese exporter that places proxy bids at USS and other auctions for overseas customers. It says it has been a JUMVEA member since 1997.
    How you buy
    Open an account, pay a deposit, choose cars from the auction listings, and they bid for you.
    Good for
    Direct access to USS, the largest auction network.
    • Dealer auctions
    • JUMVEA member
    • Export

  • BE FORWARD Live Auction

    beforward.jp
    What it is
    An auction bidding service run by BE FORWARD, one of Japan's largest used-car exporters.
    How you buy
    Register, choose auction cars online and request bids; BE FORWARD handles payment, export and shipping.
    Good for
    Buyers who want one company for auction access, stock and shipping.
    • Dealer auctions
    • Export
    • Worldwide shipping

Exporter marketplaces

Buy from stock at a fixed price

These sites list cars already bought by Japanese exporters and dealers. You pay a listed price and receive a shipping quote to your port. Classics are rarer here than at auction, but you can see the car before you commit.

  • Goo-net Exchange

    goo-net-exchange.com
    What it is
    The export edition of Goo-net, one of Japan's biggest used-car listing sites, with cars from dealers across Japan.
    How you buy
    Search, then contact the listing dealer or exporter for a price delivered to your port.
    Good for
    Classic specialist dealers, including older Skylines, Zs and Celicas.
    • Dealer stock
    • Classics

  • BE FORWARD

    beforward.jp
    What it is
    A large exporter with its own online stock and shipping to many countries.
    How you buy
    Choose a car, get a quote to your port, pay by bank transfer, and they ship.
    Good for
    Fixed-price buying. Mostly modern used cars, so classics are occasional.
    • Own stock
    • Worldwide shipping

  • SBT Japan

    sbtjapan.com
    What it is
    A Japanese exporter with online stock and sales staff who handle overseas buyers.
    How you buy
    Request a quote for a listed car, pay the invoice, and they arrange shipping.
    Good for
    Fixed-price buying with a named sales contact.
    • Own stock
    • Worldwide shipping

  • TCV

    tc-v.com
    What it is
    A marketplace, formerly tradecarview, that lists cars from many Japanese exporters.
    How you buy
    Compare listings, then deal with the exporter selling the car you choose.
    Good for

Collector auctions

For the rarest cars

The most valuable classics, such as a 2000GT or a KPGC10 GT-R, rarely go through dealer auctions. They sell through collector auction houses and specialist dealers.

  • BH Auction

    bhauction.com
    What it is
    A Japanese collector-car auction house run by BINGO Co., Ltd. It holds sales at major events such as Tokyo Auto Salon.
    How you buy
    Pre-register, then bid in person, by telephone or with an advance bid. Ask for detailed photographs if you can't attend.
    Good for
    Top-tier, well-documented Japanese classics.
    • Collector auction
    • International bidders

Before you pay

Check the company, not just the car

  • Look the company up yourself instead of following a link from a message. Fake sites copy real exporters' names and stock photos.
  • Check for membership of JUMVEA, the Japan Used Motor Vehicle Exporters Association.
  • Pay only by bank transfer to an account in the company's own name, never to a personal account or in cryptocurrency.
  • Ask for the auction sheet, the export certificate and photos of the chassis plate before you pay in full.
  • Confirm in writing that they will ship to your Indian port and supply the documents your customs broker needs for a vintage import.
